# Decisions — Motorcycle (Eliyahu's MuZ Skorpion 660)

Append-only, with dates and rationale. These initial entries were extracted from the co-work planning conversation's output in Notion (2026-07-07/08) — they were made during planning; confirm with Sinai if any feels off.

## moto-dec-001 · Recommission, don't restore
**2026-07-07 · approach**
Flush, clean, replace consumables; open the engine only if compression or valve clearances demand it. Go/No-Go gate: free-turning engine + clean matching VIN + no catastrophic rust ⇒ proceed.
- **Why:** bike stood for years but is complete; goal is safely back on the road, not concours.

## moto-dec-002 · Replace rather than revive (consumables)
**2026-07-07 · approach**
Battery ("left flat for years is scrap"), fuel lines, age-expired tyres (>5–6 yrs by DOT code), chain & sprockets as a set if worn.
- **Why:** revival attempts on aged rubber/lead-acid cost more in failures than replacement.

## moto-dec-003 · DIY with an escape hatch for the carb
**2026-07-07 · approach**
All work DIY except possibly the Teikei carb rebuild — "a shop will do this better than a first attempt — money well spent."

## moto-dec-004 · This is a family heirloom, not a profit build
**2026-07-07 · build-direction**
Market value €1,500–3,700 doesn't justify the project economically; sentimental/family value does. Spend accordingly — properly, not extravagantly.
