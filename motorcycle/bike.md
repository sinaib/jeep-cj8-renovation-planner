## Key specs (confirmed from MuZ 660 E service manual)

- **Oil:** SAE 20W-40 JASO MA, ~2.5L / ~2.6L with filter — dry sump, prime per manual
- **Plug:** NGK DPR9EA-9 (or iridium DPR9EIX-9), gap 0.9mm
- **Valves (cold):** IN 0.10–0.15mm · EX 0.15–0.20mm ✅ confirmed in manual
- **Tyres:** F 120/60-17 @ 1.9 bar · R 160/60-17 @ 2.4 bar (verify swingarm sticker)
- **Charging:** regulator 14.3–15.3V · 14V/24.5A @ 5,000rpm
- **Coolant:** ethylene glycol for aluminum engines; capacity TBD from manual
- ⚠️ The manual's "75 RH 2015, 126 links" chain is the internal **cam chain** — NOT the drive chain
- Still to extract from manual: battery Ah, drive chain size/sprocket teeth, fork oil grade/level, coolant capacity

## Model lore — known Skorpion weak points (not yet observed on this bike)

- Hard on chains & sprockets; **rear sprocket bolts loosen** (known weak point — check!)
- Electrical gremlins: tail/dash bulbs, erratic rev counter; earths/connectors = most gremlins
- Throttle cable wear (throttle must snap shut)
- Some oil consumption is normal for the 660 single
- MZ-specific cosmetic/chassis parts hard to source; engine parts easy (Yamaha commonality)

## Open questions for the owner interview

4. Registration status + papers/title in whose name; insurance history; keys
5. Who will ride it (license class), target date, budget ceiling
6. Anything already on hand (tools, parts)?
